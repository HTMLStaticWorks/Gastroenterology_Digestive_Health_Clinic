Dr. Sarah Jenkins, MD, FACG

Lead Gastroenterologist & Hepatologist

Dr. Jenkins brings over 15 years of specialized experience in complex gastrointestinal disorders. Her approach combines rigorous diagnostic precision with a deeply empathetic patient manner. She trained at Johns Hopkins and is recognized globally for her research in advanced therapeutics.

In addition to her clinical work, Dr. Jenkins serves on the advisory board of the American College of Gastroenterology, helping draft national guidelines for colon cancer screening. She is deeply passionate about women's digestive health and regularly hosts community wellness seminars.

Education & Fellowships

  • MD, Stanford University School of Medicine
  • Residency in Internal Medicine, UCSF
  • Fellowship in Gastroenterology & Hepatology, Johns Hopkins Hospital

Dr. Alan Turing, MD, PhD

Director of Endoscopy

As the Director of Endoscopy, Dr. Turing specializes in minimally invasive diagnostic and therapeutic procedures. His dual MD/PhD background means his clinical practice is informed by the absolute cutting edge of biomedical research.

Before joining Aura, Dr. Turing led several pioneering clinical trials focusing on endoscopic ultrasound (EUS) and ERCP techniques at the Mayo Clinic. He is a frequent keynote speaker at international gastroenterology symposiums and has published over 50 peer-reviewed articles.

Education & Fellowships

  • MD, Harvard Medical School
  • PhD in Biomedical Engineering, MIT
  • Residency in Internal Medicine, Massachusetts General Hospital
  • Fellowship in Advanced Therapeutic Endoscopy, Mayo Clinic
