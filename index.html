Clear Clinical Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for an appointment, procedure safety, and insurance coverage at Aura.

When should I consult a gastroenterologist instead of my primary care doctor?
You should see a gastroenterologist if you experience persistent symptoms including chronic heartburn/acid reflux that does not respond to OTC antacids, difficulty swallowing, recurrent bloating or abdominal pain, unexplained changes in bowel habits lasting over two weeks, blood in stool, unintentional weight loss, or if you have reached age 45 and need a routine preventative colon cancer screening.
Will I feel pain during a colonoscopy or upper endoscopy?
No. At Aura, all endoscopic procedures are performed with gentle, monitored anesthesia care (propofol) administered by board-certified anesthesiologists. You are completely asleep and comfortable during the examination, wake up within 10 to 15 minutes post-procedure feeling clear-headed, and have no memory of the procedure. We also use carbon dioxide (CO2) insufflation rather than air to prevent post-procedure gas pains.
How do I prepare for my procedure?
For colonoscopies, you will follow a clear liquid diet the day prior and consume a low-volume, well-tolerated bowel preparation solution prescribed by our clinic. For upper endoscopy, you simply fast for 8 hours prior to your morning appointment. Full step-by-step digital instructions, videos, and a direct clinical prep helpline are provided to every patient.
Which health insurance plans does Aura accept?
We are in-network with most major PPO, Medicare, and commercial insurance providers, including BlueCross BlueShield, Aetna, Cigna, Medicare, and UnitedHealthcare. Our dedicated insurance concierge provides complete pre-authorization and transparent benefit checks prior to your visit.
How quickly can I be seen for urgent digestive symptoms?
We maintain dedicated same-week and 48-hour priority clinical slots for urgent indications, including unexplained rectal bleeding, severe dysphagia, acute IBD flare-ups, and urgent physician referrals. Call our priority intake line directly at (555) 782-4278.
