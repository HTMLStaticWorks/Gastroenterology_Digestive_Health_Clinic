Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?
Most PPO insurance plans do not require a referral, but HMO plans typically do. We recommend checking with your insurance provider prior to your visit to ensure coverage.
What should I bring to my first consultation?
Please bring your photo ID, insurance card, a list of current medications, and any relevant medical records or previous test results related to your digestive health.
Do you offer telehealth appointments?
Yes, we offer telehealth consultations for follow-ups, test result reviews, and initial consultations where appropriate. Please select "Telehealth" when filling out your request form.
What forms of payment do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and personal checks. For specific billing inquiries, our financial concierge is happy to assist you prior to your procedure.
